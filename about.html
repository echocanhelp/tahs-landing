About TAHS

Founding

TAHS was organized in Southern California, 2013–2014. We keep both dates deliberately: a founding member's memoir records that founding president Charles Yang (楊嘉猷) convened the society in summer 2013 with it formally established by year's end, while a December 2014 letter describes the society as then being organized. The archive holds both sources side by side rather than forcing one year — the same discipline we apply to every entry in Echopedia.

The founders were elders of the Taiwanese American diaspora — engineers, physicians, publishers — who had spent decades building community institutions and wanted the record kept: interviews with pioneers, memorial services, lectures, exhibitions, and published profiles.

Publications

The society has published two volumes of 台美人菁英錄 (Taiwanese American Profiles) — collected interviews and biographical essays centered on the first generation — and in 2025 the board adopted a program to collect, interview, and publish second- and third-generation figures. See the 2023 TAHS Publication entry in Echopedia.

A note on names. TAHS is an independent 501(c)(3), distinct from the Taiwan Center (台灣會館) and from the TAH Foundation (台美史料中心, taiwaneseamericanhistory.org) — a valued sibling archive with a shared mission. The older site tahistory.org is a legacy host of record, not this organization's current site. 台美人歷史協會是獨立組織，與台灣會館、台美史料中心（taiwaneseamericanhistory.org）各自獨立；台美史料中心是與本會使命相近的姊妹檔案庫。舊站 tahistory.org 屬歷史網站，並非本會現行網站。
