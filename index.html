Mission

We document and preserve Taiwanese American history — people, congregations, and organizations — build connections across generations, and support research and education, so the next generation has a checkable record rather than rumor. 我們記錄並保存台美人的歷史——人物、教會與組織——促進世代連結，支援研究與教育，讓下一代有可查的紀錄，而不必只靠傳聞。

Not the Taiwan Center. Not the TAH Foundation (taiwaneseamericanhistory.org). The older site tahistory.org is a legacy host, not this one. 不是台灣會館，也不是台美史料中心（taiwaneseamericanhistory.org）。舊站 tahistory.org 是歷史網站，不是本站。

Roots

TAHS was organized in Southern California, 2013–2014, by elders of the Taiwanese American diaspora who had spent decades building community institutions and wanted the record kept — interviews with pioneers, memorial services, exhibitions, and two published volumes of 台美人菁英錄. The society holds 501(c)(3) status with an original IRS determination of January 13, 2014, reinstated June 15, 2024. About the founding record →
