Mission

We keep a checkable record of Taiwanese American people, congregations, and organizations, so the next generation is not left with rumor. 我們保存台美人、教會與組織的可查紀錄，讓下一代不必只靠傳聞。

Not the Taiwan Center. Not the TAH Foundation (taiwaneseamericanhistory.org). The older site tahistory.org is a legacy host, not this one. 不是台灣會館，也不是台美史料中心（taiwaneseamericanhistory.org）。舊站 tahistory.org 是歷史網站，不是本站。

Roots

TAHS was organized in Southern California, 2013–2014, by elders of the Taiwanese American diaspora who had spent decades building community institutions and wanted the record kept — interviews with pioneers, memorial services, exhibitions, and two published volumes of 台美人菁英錄. The society received its IRS 501(c)(3) determination in June 2024. About the founding record →
