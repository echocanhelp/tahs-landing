How to verify us

Search EIN 46-4005384 on the official IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search. That is the authoritative record. IRS TEOS is the only citable record. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer is a non-citable BMF convenience view; as a 990-N filer we have no public financial extract there.

Address note for vendors: our IRS record (BMF extract, 2026-10-27) shows La Habra Hgts, CA. Please confirm the current address on IRS TEOS before any address-matching check.
